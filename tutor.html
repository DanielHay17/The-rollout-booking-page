Claude isn't saving you hours yet because it's missing three things

Most people use Claude like a search box. It works better if you treat it like a very smart new starter who knows nothing about your business. It needs logins to your tools, a filing cabinet to look things up in, and your procedures written down once. Here's what that looks like when it's set up:

Your toolsXero, your inbox, your job or ERP software, Drive
Claude + your proceduresWritten once as "skills"
Work preparedBank lines coded, schedules checked, reports drafted
You approveNothing posts without you

Setting it up yourself means picking the right way to connect each system, getting permissions right and writing procedures Claude actually follows. It's easy to get wrong and give up, which is why a tutor helps.
