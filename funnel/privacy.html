Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026

The Rollout and Phlo are run by PHLOING PTY LTD in Australia. This page covers what this site (therollout.ai) collects and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you subscribe.
  • If you answer the questions after subscribing: where you are with AI, where the manual work is, your team size, first name, company website and mobile number. Everything except your email is optional.
  • Which ad, post or link brought you here, and which pages of this site you visited.
  • If you book a call, the details you give the booking calendar.

What we do with it

  • Send you The Rollout. Every issue has an unsubscribe link.
  • Prepare for and follow up on calls, which can include calling or texting the mobile you gave us.
  • Work out which ads and posts bring readers, so we spend on the ones that work.

We don't sell your details.

Who else handles it

Cloudflare (this site and our customer records), beehiiv (the newsletter), iClosed (call bookings), Slack (alerts to our team) and Meta (see below). Some of them store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

Cookies and Meta

This site uses the Meta pixel. Third parties, including Meta, may use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to collect or receive information from this site and elsewhere on the internet, and use it to measure ads and to show you ads. When you've given us your email, name or mobile, the pixel sends Meta a scrambled (hashed) copy so it can match your visit to your Meta account.

The site also remembers in your browser which link brought you here, for 30 days, so a later visit is credited to the right place.

To opt out of ads based on this, change your ad preferences in Facebook or Instagram (Accounts Centre, then Ad preferences), or use aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.eu. You can also block or clear cookies in your browser's settings.

Seeing, fixing or deleting your details

Reply to any Rollout email, or message Daniel Hay on LinkedIn, and we'll show you what we hold, correct it or delete it. If you're not happy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
